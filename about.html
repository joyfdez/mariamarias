About

A creative studio exploring color, pattern and storytelling.

Maria Mendez is a graphic designer, textile artist and illustrator creating visual stories through color, print and composition.

Rooted in textile design and pattern, the work explores the connection between objects, places and culture – blending handcrafted sensibility with bold contemporary design.
