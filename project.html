A comprehensive visual identity system developed for Art Basel's Miami Beach edition. The project encompassed print collateral, environmental graphics, and a flexible brand language adaptable across all touchpoints of the fair.

Working directly with the curatorial team, the system balances institutional authority with the energy of the art world — creating materials that frame without competing, and guide without imposing.
